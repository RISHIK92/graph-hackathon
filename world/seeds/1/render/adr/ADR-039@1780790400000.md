## Context

Loyalty Rewards needs predictable latency as traffic grows through the year.

The current setup for Loyalty Rewards makes on-call debugging slow and manual.

## Decision

We will use Kafka for Loyalty Rewards.

## Consequences

The owning team updates the Loyalty Rewards runbook and the on-call notes.
