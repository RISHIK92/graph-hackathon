## Context

Pricing Engine needs predictable latency as traffic grows through the year.

DynamoDB is already operated by other teams, so support costs stay low.

## Decision

We will use DynamoDB for Pricing Engine.

## Consequences

The owning team updates the Pricing Engine runbook and the on-call notes.
