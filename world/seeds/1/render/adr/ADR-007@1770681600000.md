## Context

Postgres Main could not sustain Ledger write volume during month-end close.

Aurora gives Ledger storage autoscaling and replica failover in under a minute.

Moving Ledger off Postgres Main frees the shared cluster for Reporting workloads.

## Decision

We will move Ledger from Postgres Main to Aurora once the cutover runbook has been rehearsed.

## Consequences

Payments and Infra share the cutover runbook, and Reporting reads from the Aurora reader endpoint.
