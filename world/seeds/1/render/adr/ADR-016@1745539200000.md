## Context

Moving to ClickHouse lets Billing-v2 scale without hand-tuned capacity.

Billing-v2 needs predictable latency as traffic grows through the year.

## Decision

We will use ClickHouse for Billing-v2.

## Consequences

The owning team updates the Billing-v2 runbook and the on-call notes.
