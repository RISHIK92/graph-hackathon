## Context

The current setup for Discovery Revamp makes on-call debugging slow and manual.

Terraform is already operated by other teams, so support costs stay low.

## Decision

We will use Terraform for Discovery Revamp.

## Consequences

The owning team updates the Discovery Revamp runbook and the on-call notes.
