## Context

Chargeback Defense needs predictable latency as traffic grows through the year.

Incidents on Chargeback Defense last quarter traced back to the existing approach.

## Decision

We will use Envoy for Chargeback Defense.

## Consequences

The owning team updates the Chargeback Defense runbook and the on-call notes.
