## Context

Session Store needs predictable latency as traffic grows through the year.

Moving to OpenTelemetry lets Session Store scale without hand-tuned capacity.

## Decision

We will use OpenTelemetry for Session Store.

## Consequences

The owning team updates the Session Store runbook and the on-call notes.
