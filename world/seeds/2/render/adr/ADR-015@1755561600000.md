## Context

Geo Lookup needs predictable latency as traffic grows through the year.

The current setup for Geo Lookup makes on-call debugging slow and manual.

## Decision

We will use gRPC for Geo Lookup.

## Consequences

The owning team updates the Geo Lookup runbook and the on-call notes.
