## Context

DynamoDB is already operated by other teams, so support costs stay low.

Incidents on Wallet Launch last quarter traced back to the existing approach.

## Decision

We will use DynamoDB for Wallet Launch.

## Consequences

The owning team updates the Wallet Launch runbook and the on-call notes.
