## Context

Store Locator needs predictable latency as traffic grows through the year.

Incidents on Store Locator last quarter traced back to the existing approach.

## Decision

We will use Envoy for Store Locator.

## Consequences

The owning team updates the Store Locator runbook and the on-call notes.
