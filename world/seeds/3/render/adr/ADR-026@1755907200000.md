## Context

The current setup for Store Locator makes on-call debugging slow and manual.

Moving to Elasticsearch lets Store Locator scale without hand-tuned capacity.

## Decision

We will use Elasticsearch for Store Locator.

## Consequences

The owning team updates the Store Locator runbook and the on-call notes.
