## Context

The current setup for Billing-v2 makes on-call debugging slow and manual.

Billing-v2 needs predictable latency as traffic grows through the year.

## Decision

We will use OpenTelemetry for Billing-v2.

## Consequences

The owning team updates the Billing-v2 runbook and the on-call notes.
