## Context

Moving to Kafka lets Billing-v2 scale without hand-tuned capacity.

Incidents on Billing-v2 last quarter traced back to the existing approach.

## Decision

We will use Kafka for Billing-v2.

## Consequences

The owning team updates the Billing-v2 runbook and the on-call notes.
